Customer Performance Dashboard – Data Visualization Project

This Power BI dashboard provides a dynamic and insightful view into customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and revenue distribution. It was designed to help stakeholders make informed decisions by surfacing patterns across customer segments.

📊 Key Metrics & Visual Insights

  • Total Revenue: $307.09M
  • Revenue Distribution by Gender:
    • Male: $152.61M (49.70%)
    • Female: $154.48M (50.30%)
  • Total Customers: 18,484
  • Average Age: 45
  • Customers with Children: 71%
  • Customers without Children: 29%

🌍 Geographic Breakdown

  • Top Country (Customers with Children): United States – 7,819 customers, $77.42M revenue (50% Male / 50% Female)
  • Top Country (Customers without Children): Australia – 3,591 customers, $39.67M revenue (51% Male / 49% Female)
